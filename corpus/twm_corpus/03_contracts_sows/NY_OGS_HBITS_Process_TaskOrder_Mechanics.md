## What HBITS Is
Pre-established contractor network for procuring hourly-based IT services for NYS Authorized Users, managed by OGS.

## Task Order Process (8 steps)
1. Internal/control-agency approvals
2. Task Order Request Form (Form 1): job title, mandatory + requested qualifications, region, duration, up to 5 positions
3. OGS assigns Task Order number, distributes Form 1 + Candidate Response Form (Form 2) to all active contractors
4. Contractors submit Form 2 candidates (max 2 per contractor per position)
5. OGS preliminary review within 3 business days (mandatory quals + cost methodology)
6. Preliminary Technical Evaluation (Form 3A) within 5 business days
7. Interviews (Form 3B)
8. Selection and onboarding

## Rate Mechanics
- **Hourly Bill Rates:** pre-determined, non-negotiable, from Attachment 1 Pricing Schedules; fixed for the engagement
- **Hourly Wage Rates:** contractor must pay candidate at least the wage rate bid
- **Administrative fee:** contractor pays 0.75% of all sales (quarterly: May 15 / Aug 15 / Nov 15 / Feb 15)
- **Regions:** three home-base geographic regions (Attachment 4)
- **Engagement duration:** 2-30 months; no optional extensions; replacements get no wage increase/extension

## Cost Methodology (rate-ceiling formula per posting)
| Positions | Valid responses | Max qualifying bill rate |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5+ | Greatest of: 5th lowest rate, Median + 5%, Median + 1 SD |
| 2 | 10+ | Greatest of: 10th lowest rate, Median + 5%, Median + 1 SD |
| 3 | 15+ | Greatest of: 15th lowest rate, Median + 5%, Median + 1 SD |
| 4 | 20+ | Greatest of: 20th lowest rate, Median + 5%, Median + 1 SD |
| 5 | 25+ | Greatest of: 25th lowest rate, Median + 5%, Median + 1 SD |
(Below-threshold response counts: OGS determines pass/cancel.)

## Other Rules
- Standard response window 10 business days (expedited 5); 60% of requested qualifications required to advance; non-compete verification required.
