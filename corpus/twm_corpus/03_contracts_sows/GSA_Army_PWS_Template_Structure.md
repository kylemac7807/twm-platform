## PART 1: GENERAL INFORMATION
1.1 Service description & contractor responsibilities; 1.2 Background; 1.3 Objectives; 1.4 Services/accomplishments required; 1.5 Performance period & option years; 1.6.1 Quality Control Program; 1.6.2 QASP; 1.6.3 Holiday work; 1.6.4 Business hours/continuity; 1.6.5 Performance location; 1.6.6 Contract type determination; 1.6.7 Security (incl. 1.6.7.1 property safeguarding, 1.6.7.2 key control, 1.6.7.3 lock combinations); 1.6.8 Certifications; 1.6.9 Post-award conference; 1.6.10 COR authority; 1.6.11 Key personnel; 1.6.12 Employee identification; 1.6.13 Travel (JTR); 1.6.14 Other Direct Costs preapproval; 1.6.15 Government data/deliverable ownership; 1.6.16 OCI; 1.6.17 Phase-in/phase-out.

## PART 2: DEFINITIONS & ACRONYMS
Contractor, Contracting Officer, Defective Service, Deliverable, Key Personnel, Quality Control; acronyms COR, QASP, PWS, OCI, CONUS, FAR.

## PART 3: GOVERNMENT FURNISHED PROPERTY/EQUIPMENT/SERVICES
3.1 Services; 3.2 Facilities; 3.3 Utilities; 3.4 Equipment; 3.5 Materials.

## PART 4: CONTRACTOR FURNISHED ITEMS
4.1 Everything not in Part 3; 4.2 Security clearances; 4.3 Materials; 4.4 Equipment.

## PART 5: SPECIFIC TASKS
5.1 Basic services; 5.2+ individual task statements; 5.3 Contractor Manpower Reporting (contract number, period, estimated direct labor hours and dollars; annual reporting due Oct 31).

## PART 6: APPLICABLE PUBLICATIONS
ARs (e.g., AR 25-2, AR 530-1), FAR/DFARS, local policy.

## PART 7: ATTACHMENTS / TECHNICAL EXHIBITS
- **TE 1 Performance Requirements Summary:** Performance Objective ("shall" statement + PWS ref) | Standard/Performance Threshold | Method of Surveillance (100% inspection, random sampling, periodic, validated complaint)
- **TE 2 Deliverables Schedule:** Deliverable (+PWS ref) | Frequency | # Copies | Medium/Format | Submit To — e.g., "DoD Business Process Report (PWS 5.5) | by 5th monthly | 1 orig + 2 copies | MS Word on CD | [Activity], ATTN: [POC]"
- **TE 3 Estimated Workload Data:** Item | Labor Category (e.g., Contract Manager) | Estimated Hours

## Contract Type & Invoicing Guidance
- Contract type placeholder (determined by CCE and customer)
- Non-FFP contracts: no reimbursement when government facility closed
- Travel: government approval, NTE amounts; ODCs pre-approved by CO

Authoritative federal PWS skeleton: performance-standard tables (objective/threshold/surveillance), deliverables schemas, and the labor-category-by-hours workload exhibit — a structural template for recognizing SOW/PWS anatomy and QASP-linked pricing in services contracts.
