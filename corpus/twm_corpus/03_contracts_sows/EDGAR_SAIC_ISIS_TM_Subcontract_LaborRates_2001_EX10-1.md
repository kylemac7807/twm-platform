## Key Commercial Terms
- Not-to-Exceed Value: $8,928,147.00; Period of Performance: 2001-05-21 to 2003-09-30
- Initial funding: $1,839,422.00 (anticipated through 2001-11-30) — incremental funding construct
- Invoicing: monthly, duplicate, detailed labor and material breakdowns; Net 30; 5% withhold until closeout
- Material handling: 15% of direct material and other direct costs; materials/travel at actual cost per GAAP
- Burn-rate notification: required if cumulative labor >10% above even distribution or monthly hours >15% above budget
- Change orders: 20-day notification for adjustment claims; default cure: 10 days
- Insurance: $1,000,000 per occurrence minimums
