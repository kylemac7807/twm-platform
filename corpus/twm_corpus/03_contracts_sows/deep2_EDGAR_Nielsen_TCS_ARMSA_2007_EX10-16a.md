## (1) Identity
- **Title:** Amended and Restated Master Services Agreement
- **Parties:** Tata America International Corporation (TCS America) + Tata Consultancy Services Limited (TCSL) with ACNielsen (US), Inc.
- One of the largest India-offshore ITO/BPO deals of its era (later expanded to ~$2.25B through 2025 per Nielsen's 2017 8-K).

## (3) Key Commercial Constructs
- **SOW mechanics (3.5(a)):** each SOW describes services, states the maximum number of TCS Personnel Nielsen will be billed for, becomes part of the Agreement; three standard SOW templates by delivery model
- **Minimum Commitment Amount (Art. 17):** minimum purchase volume obligation (amounts redacted), enforcement in 17.1-17.4
- **Off-Shore Leverage formula (3.2):** Off-Shore % = total billed hours performed Off-Shore / (on-shore + Off-Shore hours) x 100, targets by quarter/year in Schedule F; missing targets is NOT a breach and cannot increase Charges
- **Charging (Art. 18 + Sched. C):** Rate Card by resource type and year; T&M (18.2); Fixed Price (18.11); Baseline Service Charges — forecasted monthly fee for resource baselines (18.1); expense pass-through (18.3); **Most Favored Customer clause (18.12, redacted)**
- **Service levels:** Tier One QoS metrics + Critical Service Levels, adjustment mechanism (8.5), remedies (8.3-8.7)
- **Governance:** Executive Steering Committee, governance model (Sched. J), change control (12.2)
- **Termination:** payment on termination/expiration (26.6, amounts redacted); Termination-Expiration Assistance per Schedule O
