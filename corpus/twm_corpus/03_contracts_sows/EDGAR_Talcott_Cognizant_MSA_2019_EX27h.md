## (3) SOW Issuance & Charging Framework
- Each SOW identifies the MSA, is subject to its terms, executed by both parties; SOWs survive MSA termination
- Charges per SOW; invoices monthly; payment 30 calendar days from receipt; USD default
- **COLA:** "If agreed to in a Statement of Work, charges under a Statement of Work may be subject to cost of living adjustments"
- Prorated charges on calendar-month basis; Pass-Through Expenses set forth in SOW; change charges per Change Control Procedures
- Tax allocation: Talcott pays sales/use/excise/VAT/GST (excl. Cognizant net income taxes); each party pays property taxes on own property
- Change absorption rule: change accommodated within existing resources at no cost to Cognizant → no Charge increase

## (4) Key Commercial Terms
- **Term:** 7-year initial from 2019-09-01; automatic 1-year renewals; 120-day non-renewal notice
- Service Commencement upon completion of Transition
- **Key Personnel:** max 5% of dedicated employees designated Key Cognizant Positions; consent required for removal/replacement within first 6 months or contracted period
- Service Levels per SOW with monitoring tools and agreed penalties
- Invoice disputes: pay undisputed portion; notify within 30 days; late payment bears Interest
- DR/BC: documented, tested plans for critical Services
