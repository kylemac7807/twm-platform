## Key Commercial Terms

### Contract Term
- Initial Term: Three years from Effective Date (August 2, 2001)
- Optional Term: FNIS may extend for additional three years (30-day notice before expiration)
- Renewal Terms: Up to three one-year automatic renewals (FNIS may decline with 90 days' notice; MGEN with 6 months' notice)

### Invoicing and Payment
- Payment by wire transfer; due on or before the 30th calendar day after receipt of invoice
- Late payment interest: lesser of 0.9% per month or maximum lawful rate
- Invoice must detail pricing sufficiently for validation; reference applicable project; include narrative of services rendered
- No expense reimbursement unless specified or agreed in advance

### Rate Escalation
- MGEN may adjust hourly rates and annual Maintenance/Support charges annually
- Initial Term cap: cumulative percentage increase in U.S. CPI since last adjustment
- If MGEN demonstrates industry-wide increases exceed CPI, parties negotiate in good faith
- Renewal Terms: MGEN may adjust to then-current standard rates

### Most Favored Customer Pricing
- Prices charged to FNIS no higher than those offered to any direct FNIS competitor for substantially similar services; lower competitor pricing applies retroactively

### Termination
- Nonpayment: MGEN may terminate if default not cured within 15 days (escrow alternative available)
- Cause: material/repeated default not substantially cured within 15 days; no termination charge for cause
- Transition assistance post-termination: up to termination date plus six months, billed at Consulting Services rates

### Other
- Liability cap: three months' compensation (carve-outs: confidentiality, payment, IP non-infringement, GLBA compliance)
- Insurance minimum: $50,000,000
- Software escrow: source code deposited within 30 days, refreshed annually
- Service level failures: remediation plan required; failure deemed material breach
