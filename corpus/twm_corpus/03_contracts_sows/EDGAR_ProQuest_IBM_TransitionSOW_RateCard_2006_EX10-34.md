## Key Commercial Terms
- Scope: staff augmentation — IBM US, India onshore, and offshore resources for application management, production support, knowledge transfer during 90-day wind-down (2006-02-01 to 2006-05-01)
- Billing: T&M plus fixed PMO fees; minimum 40 billable hours/week per resource
- Payment: Net 30; 1% monthly late fee
- Volume commitment: ProQuest must purchase minimum $2M new IBM services in 2006 and $2M in 2007 (excluding maintenance)
- One-time payment: $4,029,689 due by 2006-03-31 (Sec. 2.8.4)
- Personnel release: 30-day advance notice; resource roll-off schedule within 10 days
- Termination: 60 days' notice; fees through termination plus reasonable wind-down charges
