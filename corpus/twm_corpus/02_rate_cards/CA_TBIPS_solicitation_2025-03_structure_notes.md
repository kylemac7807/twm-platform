# Canada PSPC TBIPS — Solicitation 2025-03 (Programmer/Analyst resources) — structure notes
Source: https://canadabuys.canada.ca/sites/default/files/webform/tender_notice/70780/tbips-solicitation--2025-03-programmer-analysts-resources_en.pdf
Issuer: Government of Canada (TBIPS supply arrangement solicitation, 2025). Extracted via WebFetch 2026-09-01.
Content: no published rates (rates are bid into Annex B Basis of Payment), but documents TBIPS pricing mechanics:
- Categories sought: Stream 1 A.6 Programmer/Software Analyst Level 2 (API Developer; Blazor Developer); Stream 4 B.3 Business Consultant Level 2 (QA Engineer); B.1 Business Analyst Level 2 (Scrum Master).
- Basis of payment: fixed all-inclusive PER DIEM rates, taxes extra; partial days prorated on a 7.5-hour workday.
- Rate rules: bid rate may not increase >5% between contract periods; Level 3 rate must be >= Level 2 rate for same category.
Why useful to TWM: authoritative description of Canadian federal per-diem rate-card structure (category code x level x period), directly analogous to client rate cards.
