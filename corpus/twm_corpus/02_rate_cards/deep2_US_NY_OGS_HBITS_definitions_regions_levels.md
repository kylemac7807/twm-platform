# NY OGS HBITS (Award 23158) — Region, Skill Level and Job Title Definitions
VINTAGE/EFFECTIVE: HBITS award 23158, contract period 2024-07-01 to 2029-06-30 (current). Extracted 2026-09-01.
Source: https://ogs.ny.gov/procurement/hbits-definitions-23158 ("Job Titles, Skill Levels, Regions - Attachment 4")
Issuer: New York State Office of General Services. License: NYS public procurement record.

## Regions (companion to US_NY_OGS_HBITS_avg_hourly_bill_rates_2026.csv)
- Region 1: all NY counties not in Region 2 or 3 (upstate)
- Region 2: Mid-Hudson — Dutchess, Orange, Putnam counties
- Region 3: NYC Metropolitan — Nassau, Rockland, Suffolk, Westchester, Bronx, Kings, New York, Queens, Richmond counties

## Skill levels
| Level | Experience | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|
| Junior | 12-36 months | entry-level |
| Mid-Level | 36-60 months | works independently without assistance |
| Senior | 60-84 months | works independently, provides guidance to others |
| Expert | 84+ months | provides guidance to large teams, industry recognition |
