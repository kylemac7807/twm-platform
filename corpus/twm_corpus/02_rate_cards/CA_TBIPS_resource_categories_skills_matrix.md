Content: TBIPS resource category taxonomy — 7 streams, ~93 categories, each offered at Level 1/2/3.

## Level definitions (uniform across streams)
- Level 1 (Junior): < 5 years of experience
- Level 2 (Intermediate): 5 to < 10 years of experience
- Level 3 (Senior): 10+ years of experience
(Some categories allow Level 3 via 5+ years plus professional certification — flagged below as "or cert".)

## Rate mechanics (from TBIPS solicitations)
Per-diem based: fixed all-inclusive per diem rates, 7.5-hour workday, prorated partial days; Level 3 rate >= Level 2 rate; <=5% escalation between periods. Awarded per-diem rates are NOT centrally published (bid-specific; see manifest "Leads").

Why useful to TWM: this is the Government of Canada's IT labor category/level taxonomy — the canonical role x seniority grid Canadian suppliers price against; directly maps to bank/telecom rate card structures.
