# Texas DIR ITSAC — Appendix D: Job Category / Title Descriptions (2024 generation)
Source: https://www.cgi.com/sites/default/files/2024-10/dir-cpo-5498-appendix-d-itsac-job-category-title-descriptions.docx.pdf
(Official Texas DIR contract appendix, DIR-CPO-5498; identical Appendix D used across all ITSAC contracts. DIR-hosted copies at dir.texas.gov / txdir.widen.net were unreachable from this environment.)
Issuer: Texas Department of Information Resources. Extracted via WebFetch 2026-09-01 (condensed descriptions).
Levels: each title is contracted at Intern Level 1-3 and Level 1-3 (see companion NTE rates CSV). Licensing: Texas public procurement record.

### DevOps Engineer
Works with IT developers to facilitate better coordination among operations, development, and testing functions by automating and streamlining integration and deployment processes.

### Systems Analyst
Understands business objectives and problems, identifies alternative solutions, performs studies and cost/benefit analysis of alternatives. Analyzes user requirements, procedures, and problems to automate processing or improve existing computer systems.

### Application Architect
Responsible for designing, developing and implementing applications for computer systems; directs the development process from conception to completion and oversees technical staff efforts.

### Business Analyst
Reviews, analyzes, and evaluates business systems and user needs. Formulates systems to parallel overall business strategies; experienced with business process reengineering and identifying new applications of technology.

### Cloud Solutions Architect
Directs architectural aspects of a cloud brokering team across all aspects of IT and the business; responsible for planning and engineering the organization's cloud computing infrastructure.

### Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning Engineer
Enhances data collection procedures for building analytics and machine learning systems; generates, maintains and delivers detailed and accurate reporting on information gathered by AI systems.

### Developer/Programmer Analyst
Plans, develops, tests, and documents computer programs applying knowledge of programming techniques. Evaluates user requests and formulates plans outlining steps required to develop programs.

### Enterprise Architect
Translates client business requirements into specific systems, applications or process designs; primary expert to construct the specific solution, scope document, risk profile.

### Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) Business Analyst
Assists with developing and maintaining ERP applications by customizing and configuring workflow to ensure proper integration of the server/client application.

### ERP Developer
Reviews, analyzes, and modifies programming systems including encoding, testing, and debugging to support the organization's ERP applications; ensures software can be completely integrated into the ERP system.

### Mobile Applications Developer
Creates, maintains and implements source code to develop mobile apps and programs that meet the needs and requirements of the clients.

### Technical Writer
Develops and maintains user and technical documentation; understands the user's view of applications and puts procedures in a logical sequence.

### Digital Product Manager
Builds, implements and manages software or digital products; governs a product from its inception to customer delivery.

### Software Engineer
Develops software solutions by studying information needs, conferring with users, and studying systems flow, data usage, and work processes.

### Software Developer
Researches, designs, implements and manages software programs, working closely with other developers and analysts.

### Database Administrator
Manages production databases, monitors performance (performance tuning), directs programmers on changes, ensures data integrity.

### Database Architect
Designs and builds relational databases; develops strategies for data acquisition and recovery; maintains centralized metadata registries aligned with strategic goals.

### Data Modeler
Translates complex business data into usable computer systems, designing databases using conceptual, logical, and physical data models.

### Data Scientist (Big Data Engineer)
Extracts value from data — fetches information from various sources, analyzes business performance patterns, builds AI tools that automate processes.

### Business Intelligence Analyst
Analyzes large data sets to identify market and business trends and communicates those trends to the company.

### Data Analyst/Report Writer
Transforms data into actionable business information — acquires data, creates regular reports, maintains organized databases.

### Front-End Web Developer/Web Designer
Website design specialist; determines webpage structure balancing functional and aesthetic design.

### Web Developer
Creates website layouts using standard HTML/CSS practices; integrates backend services and databases.

### Senior Web Developer
Communicates with clients on specifications; manages teams of designers and writers; assists recruiting/training junior developers.

### Web Administrator
Maintains hardware and software critical to the functionality of the organization's Internet/intranet; monitors performance, troubleshoots.

### QA Engineer - Automated
Designs automated testing scripts; prioritizes test scenarios and creates execution plans.

### QA/Test Manager
Leads testing teams; quality advocacy, resource planning, resolving issues impeding testing.

### QA Associate/Analyst
Investigates quality complaints, tracks problematic components, verifies logs/databases, recommends corrective actions.

### Network Engineer
Complex network engineering work — designs, implements, manages network infrastructure; installation, configuration, repair and maintenance.

### Wireless Network Engineer
Installs, configures, maintains wireless equipment per 802.11 standards and best practices for high-density WiFi.

### Network Administrator
Installs, configures, supports LAN/WAN; capacity planning; ensures network availability.

### Telecommunications Manager
Plans, builds, configures, commissions telecom equipment and networks (voice, data, satellite, microwave).

### Telecommunications Specialist/Technician
Designs video, data, voice communication systems; oversees installation and maintenance.

### Network Operations Center (NOC) Technician
Oversees complex network components; daily troubleshooting from a central location where IT support technicians administer, monitor, fix, and maintain client networks.

### Data Security Analyst
Installs security programs protecting sensitive data (firewalls, encryption); maintains systems.

### Security Administrator
Configures security systems, analyzes security requirements, monitors network traffic, creates policies against unauthorized access.

### Network Security Analyst
Protects networks against cyber-attacks; monitors logs, identifies vulnerabilities, develops mitigation strategies.

### Network Security Engineer
Provisions, deploys, configures, administers network/security hardware and software: firewalls, routers, switches, monitoring tools, VPNs.

### Information Security Manager
Coordinates and executes security policies and controls; assesses vulnerabilities; manages data and network security; investigates violations.

### IT Auditor
Identifies weaknesses in systems/networks; assesses internal controls and risks; develops action plans against breaches.

### Project Manager
Plans, budgets, oversees, documents all project aspects; 3+ years progressive experience with systems integration and project delivery.

### Project Lead
Planning, coordination, requirements gathering, business analysis, specifications, status reporting for moderate to complex solutions.

### Program Manager
Organizes programs aligned with organizational missions; develops initiatives supporting strategic direction; manages long-term goals with budgeting.

### Agile Scrum Master
Monitors scrum processes and meetings; increases team efficiency; ensures Definition of Done compliance.

### Agile Coach
Trains corporate teams on agile methodology, guides implementation; 4+ years experience.

### Support Technician
Maintains, analyzes, troubleshoots, repairs computer systems, hardware, peripherals; manages user accounts.

### Help Desk (Technician)
Technical assistance for user issues via phone, email, in person; tracks issues; documents solutions.

### Product Support Analyst
Evaluates failures/bugs/systemic problems; troubleshoots and diagnoses; contributes to knowledge bases.

### Business Continuity Analyst
Analyzes potential risks/losses in outages or disasters; designs, tests, implements recovery plans.

### Site Reliability Engineer
Manages availability, latency, performance, efficiency, change management, monitoring, emergency response; software engineering applied to system administration.

### Instructor Trainer
Develops training materials and delivers instruction on IT systems and technologies.

### Change Management Manager/Organizational Change Management (OCM)
Manages organizational transitions during IT changes: stakeholder communication, resistance mitigation, implementation.

### Process Improvement Manager
Identifies inefficiencies in IT operations, recommends and implements process enhancements.

### Communications Coordinator
Creates and distributes IT-related communications and marketing materials.

### Digital Marketing Analyst
Analyzes digital marketing performance metrics, develops online strategies, optimizes campaigns.

### Web Content Specialist/Manager
Manages website content creation, updates, maintenance.

### IT Contract Manager
Oversees IT contract negotiation, execution, administration; vendor relationships; compliance.

### IT Contract Administrator/Technician
Processes contract documentation, maintains records, tracks vendor performance.

### IT Procurement Specialist/Technician
Identifies procurement needs, evaluates vendors, negotiates pricing, manages purchasing.
