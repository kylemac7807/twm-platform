## Notes

- Offshore tiers: PRESENT for all categories (£960-£2,075/day) — PwC's offshore rates alone exceed many competitors' UK rates.
- Rack rates are dramatically above peers (junior developer £2,120 vs Capgemini £526, Kyndryl £690, Atos £692). Consistent with PwC's GC14 posture of high published rates with negotiated call-off discounts.
- vs G-Cloud 14 (May 2024): PwC GC14 cloud-support card topped out around £3,500-£3,700 at senior grades; GC15 top band is £3,940 — a modest headline uplift, with junior rack rates up more.
