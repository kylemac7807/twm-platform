- Offshore tiers: PRESENT across all categories; offshore discount ~72-77% at junior/mid bands (e.g., £840 UK vs £195 offshore) — one of the steepest arbitrage spreads in GC15.
- vs G-Cloud 14 (May 2024): KPMG GC14 SFIA rates ran roughly £700-£2,600 (SFIA 2-7 onshore); GC15 bands £840-£2,808 imply a ~8-20% uplift at equivalent seniority.
