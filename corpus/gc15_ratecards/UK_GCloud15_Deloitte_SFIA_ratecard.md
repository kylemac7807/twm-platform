- **Discounts:** time-limited discounts may be offered, made available to all Digital Marketplace buyers
- **Licensing note:** Public UK government procurement document (CCS Digital Marketplace), Crown copyright / OGL context.
- **Structure note:** Deloitte prices by Civil Service grade-equivalence bands (not SFIA 1-7), applied across 222 named roles in the standard GC15 role families (Architecture, Chief digital/data, Cyber security, Data, IT operations, Product/delivery, QAT, Software development, User-centred design). ## Notes

- Offshore tiers: PRESENT (separate 9-band offshore card, £355-£840/day). Offshore discount vs UK Standard roughly 53-59% at equivalent bands.
- vs G-Cloud 14 (May 2024): GC14 Deloitte SFIA card ran roughly £600-£2,025 across SFIA 1-7 (onshore); GC15 UK Standard runs £765-£2,125, i.e., mid-single-digit to ~10% uplift over two years at the top; entry bands up more sharply (£600 -> £765).
- The 222-role mapping in the PDF maps each Government Digital and Data role/level to one of the bands above; the band tables are the complete price list.
