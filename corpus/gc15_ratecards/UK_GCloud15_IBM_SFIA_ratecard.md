## Notes

- Offshore tiers: PRESENT across all categories. IBM's offshore ladder is uniform (£350/£400/£500/£600/£765/£1,065) regardless of category — offshore discount ~55-70%.
- IBM prices architecture/design/data roles at a premium band (£920-£2,745 UK) but delivery/ops at mid-market (£595-£1,445).
- vs G-Cloud 14 (May 2024): IBM GC14 SFIA ran roughly £550-£2,000 onshore for equivalent grades; GC15 shows ~10-15% uplift at the top and explicit offshore tiers, which IBM did not publish per-role in GC14.
