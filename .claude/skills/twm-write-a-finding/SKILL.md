# Write a finding

Every finding has: **type** (one of the versioned discrepancy or benchmark-gap types), **tranche** (Contractual, Operational, Market Alignment, by the stacking rule), **amount** per hour and per year, **volume** it applies to (hours or headcount), **confidence**, and an **evidence pack**: the source documents with page positions, the contracted rate, the vendor's other rates, the benchmark cut with its cohort size and window, and the rule version.

Rules: observed and projected shown separately and labelled; a missing reference gives zero with the reason, never a negative; below the engagement's minimum annual value a finding is recorded but not surfaced in executive views; a role billed without a contracted rate is its own finding; nothing reaches an executive view below the confidence threshold unless an analyst confirmed it.

Write the one-line summary so a CIO understands it without the table: "Senior developers on SOW 14 are billed $140/hr against a contracted $125; 12 people; $374K a year."
