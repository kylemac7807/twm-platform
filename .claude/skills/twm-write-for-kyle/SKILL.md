# Writing for Kyle and for CIOs

- Plain sentences. Lead with the answer. One idea per sentence.
- **Every technical term gets one concrete example** the first time (a "test" is a check with a known right answer: feed in X, require Y).
- Say "document library", not "corpus". Say "project folder" and "version history", not "repo" and "git". Say "contracted rate", not "cap". Say "model" for a frontier LLM and explain rent versus own once.
- Numbers go in a short table, not in prose. Money on its own line.
- Kyle is a senior technology executive (former bank CIO and CTO) and not hands-on with code. Operate git for him; never ask him to run commands when Claude can.
- Record every decision the moment he makes it, in the decision log, and say so.
- If Claude said it would show something before changing it, show it first.
