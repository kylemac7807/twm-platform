## Found on the first real run, for Kyle

1. **A confidence floor for terms: 0.75, not 0.90.** The model puts 0.8 on most clauses (40 of 61) and 0.9 or more on only 6; the 0.90 money floor would flag 92 percent of the terms although the software proved every quote on its page. The floor for a term is about the typing of the condition, not the number, so `TERM_CONFIDENCE_FLOOR = 0.75` in `ledger.py` (my addition). With it, 33 of 61 are recorded and 28 flagged (17 of those because the inventory has no key yet). Say if you want 0.90.
2. **The inventory is already growing, as designed.** 41 conditions came back as `other` across the three documents, each with the model's one-line note, stored flagged and queued for naming. Candidates worth a key, grouped the way the inventory is:
   - B, how time is counted: partial months prorated (Texas p13; Michigan p287, 1/30 per day); no billing during sprint pauses (Texas p19); a cap of 250 on-call hours (Michigan p270).
   - D, discounts, credits and commitments: a $400,000 credit if delivered hours fall below 93 percent of planned capacity (Michigan p9); a 10 percent holdback on acceptance (Texas p18); maintenance charges capped at 12 percent of development charges (Texas p19); liquidated damages for late deliverables (Michigan p278); a vendor price reduction passes automatically to the client (Michigan p286); rebates on pass-throughs passed to the client (Texas p22); extraordinary-event savings passed to the client (Texas p11).
   - F, invoicing mechanics: invoiced in arrears (Texas p13); invoices only after the month's deliverables are accepted (Michigan p89); invoices no more often than monthly (Michigan p170); right to invoice waived after four invoice cycles (Texas p14); progress payments on verified progress (Michigan p293); milestone payments on acceptance as percentages of a fixed total (Michigan pp 101, 190, 278).
   - E, scope and cost allocation: re-performance and defect correction at no charge (Texas p7; Michigan pp 104, 284); who bears compliance, force-majeure and transfer costs (Texas master agreement pp 30, 80, 90); fixed monthly fee changes by change notice (Michigan pp 24, 92); updates and emergency support included (Michigan p268).
